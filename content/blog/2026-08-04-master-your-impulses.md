## Master Your Impulses

> No man is free who is not master of himself.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 4.1.62

That moment when the WOD gets tough, when the bar feels heavy, or the clock ticks too slow. Your mind screams for you to stop, to scale back, to just take a break. This isn't just about physical limits; it's a battle against your own impulses, a test of your mental fortitude right there on the gym floor.

True strength isn't just in lifting more or running faster, but in the discipline to push past the comfortable, to choose the hard thing when your instinct says otherwise. Recognize those urges, acknowledge them, then consciously choose to act differently. You are in control.

**Today's challenge:** Today, when you hit that wall in your workout, push for just one more rep, one more calorie, or one more second than your mind tells you you can.
