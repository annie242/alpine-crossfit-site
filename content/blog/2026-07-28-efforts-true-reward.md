## Effort's True Reward

> Difficulties strengthen the mind, as labor does the body.
>
> — Seneca, Moral Letters to Lucilius 13.4

We often seek comfort, ease, and shortcuts. But true growth, whether in the gym or in life, rarely comes from the path of least resistance. It's in the struggle, the burning muscles, the moments of doubt, that our resilience is forged.

Embrace the grind. Recognize that every challenging rep, every tough WOD, isn't just about physical gains; it's about building an unshakeable mind. You are training yourself to face adversity, not just in the box, but in every corner of your life.

**Today's challenge:** Today, when the workout gets tough, instead of wishing it were over, lean into the discomfort for one extra rep or five more seconds.
