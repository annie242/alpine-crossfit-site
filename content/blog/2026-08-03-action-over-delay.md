## Action Over Delay

> While we are postponing, life speeds by.
>
> — Seneca, Letters from a Stoic

How often do we find ourselves waiting for the 'perfect' moment, the 'right' conditions, or just feeling a bit more motivated? We tell ourselves we'll tackle that tough workout, clean up our diet, or start that new skill tomorrow. But tomorrow often brings the same hesitations, the same excuses, and the same unfulfilled intentions. Seneca reminds us that time isn't waiting.

Every moment we delay is a moment lost. In the gym, this means reps not done, strength not built, progress stalled. In life, it's opportunities missed and growth deferred. Don't let the fear of imperfection or the comfort of procrastination rob you of the present chance to act. The only way to move forward is to start, right now, with what you have.

**Today's challenge:** Today, pick one thing you've been putting off, whether it's an extra set, a mobility drill, or a difficult conversation, and tackle it first.
