## The Power of Habit

> Every habit and faculty is strengthened and increased by corresponding actions: the habit of walking by walking, the habit of running by running.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 2.18.1

Think about your best lifts, your fastest WODs, or that skill you finally nailed. They weren't born from a single heroic effort, but from countless repetitions, showing up day after day, even when it felt like nothing was changing. Each rep, each session, was a brick laid in the foundation of your capability.

This isn't just about physical strength; it's about mental fortitude. What you repeatedly do, you become. So, examine your daily actions, both inside and outside the gym, and consciously choose to reinforce the habits that build the person you want to be.

**Today's challenge:** Today, identify one small, positive habit you can commit to for just five minutes, and execute it without hesitation.
