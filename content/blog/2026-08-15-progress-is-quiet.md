## Progress Is Quiet

> Growth comes from gradual changes. To leap forward is never easy, whether for a plant or a man.
>
> — Seneca, Moral Letters to Lucilius 72.8

We often want the dramatic transformation overnight. We chase the PR that requires a miracle or the workout that leaves us completely wrecked, thinking true progress only happens at the absolute limit. Yet real adaptation rarely announces itself with fireworks. It happens quietly, rep by rep, week after week, in the mundane moments when you simply show up and do the work.

Stop waiting for lightning to strike in the box. True strength is built on consistency, not heroic outbursts, so focus on stacking small, unglamorous wins today.

**Today's challenge:** Drop the ego on your weights today and focus entirely on making every single rep look identical and crisp.
