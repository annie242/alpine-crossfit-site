## Embrace the Struggle

> Difficulties strengthen the mind, as labor does the body.
>
> — Seneca, Moral Letters to Lucilius 66.3

Think about the last time you hit a wall in a workout. That burning in your lungs, the muscles screaming for a break — that's not just physical discomfort. It's a forge, shaping not only your body but your resolve. Each rep, each moment of pushing past what you thought was possible, is an act of deliberate mental conditioning.

Just as your body adapts to the demands of the barbell, your mind grows tougher with every challenge you face, inside or outside the gym. Don't shy away from the hard stuff; lean into it. You're not just building strength; you're building an unshakeable spirit.

**Today's challenge:** Today, when you feel the urge to scale back or quit, choose to do one more rep, or hold that uncomfortable position for an extra second, and notice the mental shift.
