## Practice Makes Progress

> Virtue depends partly upon training and partly upon practice; you must learn first, and then strengthen your learning by actions.
>
> — Seneca, Moral Letters to Lucilius

Seneca understood that true strength isn't just about knowing what to do, but consistently doing it. Think of that complex lift or the brutal last minute of a WOD: you don't master it by reading about it. You master it by showing up, drilling the movement, failing, adjusting, and trying again.

This isn't about natural talent; it's about the grind. Every rep, every class, every time you push past comfort, you're not just building muscle, you're forging character. You are actively strengthening your learning through deliberate action, making virtue a habit.

**Today's challenge:** Today, pick one movement you find challenging and commit to an extra 5 minutes of focused practice on it after the WOD, even if it's just practicing the empty bar pathing working on the setup.
