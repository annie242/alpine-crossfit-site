## The Enduring Good

> If you accomplish something good with hard work, the labor passes quickly, but the good endures; if you do something shameful in pursuit of pleasure, the pleasure passes quickly, but the shame endures.
>
> — Musonius Rufus, Lectures and Sayings

That burning in your lungs, the tremor in your muscles—it's intense now, but it's fleeting. Musonius Rufus reminds us that the effort, the 'labor,' passes. What remains isn't the fleeting discomfort, but the lasting strength, the earned skill, the character forged in the fire of discipline. This isn't just about a PR; it's about building a better you.

Don't chase the easy path or the momentary relief. Focus on the enduring good, the long-term gain that comes from pushing through. The satisfaction of a hard-won session, the resilience you cultivate, these are the rewards that truly last, long after the sweat has dried. Choose the lasting good over fleeting ease.

**Today's challenge:** Today, when the WOD gets tough, remind yourself that the discomfort is temporary, but the strength you're building is for keeps.
