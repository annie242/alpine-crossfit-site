## The Inner Citadel

> If you are distressed by anything external, the pain is not due to the thing itself, but to your estimate of it; and this you have the power to revoke at any moment.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 8.47

That burning in your lungs, the shaky feeling in your muscles, the voice telling you to stop – these are just sensations, not commands. You assign the meaning to them, deciding if they are limits or just feedback. The weight on the bar, the clock ticking down, the discomfort of a new movement; none of these things inherently defeat you.

Your mind is the ultimate arbiter of your experience. You possess the power to reframe any perceived obstacle into a challenge, any pain into a signal for growth. Today, remember that your judgment, not the external event, is what truly matters. Master your internal narrative, and you master the workout.

**Today's challenge:** When the workout gets tough, identify one negative thought and consciously replace it with a positive, empowering one.
