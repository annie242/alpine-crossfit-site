## The Labor Passes, The Good Endures

> If you accomplish something good with hard work, the labor passes quickly, but the good endures.
>
> — Musonius Rufus, Lectures 6.11.1

Ten minutes into a brutal metcon, the barbell feels bolted to the floor and your lungs are burning. Every instinct screams to drop the weight, back off, and seek comfort. That desire for ease is loud, but it is entirely temporary. Once the clock stops, the discomfort vanishes almost as fast as it arrived.

What remains long after the sweat dries is the quiet confidence of knowing you didn't quit when it got heavy. Character isn't built by avoiding the friction; it is forged right inside it. Stop negotiating with the fatigue today and finish the round clean.

**Today's challenge:** Do not drop your barbell early on your final set — hold it for five extra seconds of unbroken work.
